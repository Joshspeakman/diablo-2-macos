Diablo II: native Glide / 3dfx on Metal

Choose Video -> Glide / 3dfx. The 3D graphics group also applies to Glide: perspective, display rendering, texture filtering, frame rate, width and zoom. DirectDraw, windowed mode and Direct3D remain selectable.

The guest uses the original game's Glide renderer. Native Glide entry points translate its textures, palettes, chroma, color/alpha combiners, blending, clipping and movie framebuffer transfers into Metal work. This implements the single-TMU subset Diablo II uses.

Display -> Glide Glow: Off (default), Subtle or Strong. Adds colored bloom around bright world details in Glide. Applied before the interface and gamma; menus and cinematics are excluded. This is bloom, not new dynamic lights or shadows. Off retains the original image and has no glow-pass overhead.

Validation: real Glide gameplay at 800x600 and 1066x600/display scaling, existing Direct3D gameplay, guest-ABI-to-Metal fixture, launcher preference round trips and nine runtime regression groups.
